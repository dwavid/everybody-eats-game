# Everybody Eats!

A browser side-scroller (same family as the Boulder game, `dwavid/boulder-game`): Cab the chef feeds dinner guests a food that rhymes with their name. Each guest is a level; beating the food-themed boss unlocks the food. Plain HTML/Canvas/JS, no build step, no dependencies. Keep changes small, readable and easy to test.

## Layout
Plain `<script>` files that share one global scope, loaded in this order by `index.html`:
- `levels.js`: the `LEVELS` array. One entry per guest: guest + food + rhyme, theme, `kinds` (which enemy/hazard art to use), the layout, the boss and the intro/outro dialogue. Also `GROUND`, `IS_TOUCH` and cookie helpers.
- `themes.js`: art. Per-world scenery in `THEMES` (`sky`, `far`, `ground`, `pit`, `plat`, `decor`, decor `kinds`, `wallBg`), the enemy / hazard art tables (`MINI_ART`, `THROWER_ART`, `DROP_ART`, `drawPuddle`, `drawSlicer`, `drawGeyser`, `drawBoat`), and the food icons (`FOODS`).
- `themes2.js`: the same kind of art for worlds 4-6 (Tomato Garden, Cacciatore Woods, Baloney Factory): more enemy/thrower/drop kinds, `drawSpikes`, `drawSpring`, `drawBelt`, `drawGeyserPress`, and the `garden` / `woods` / `factory` themes. It registers into the tables in `themes.js` (`Object.assign`), so it must load after it.
- `bosses.js`: `BOSSES` (`peel`, `salami`, `gravy`, `tomato`, `chicken`, `baloney`). Each boss has its own state machine (`step`), projectiles (`proj`, `drawProj`), art (`draw`) and hitbox rules.
- `music.js`: the music engine (loaded before `game.js`). Chiptune big-band swing: each track in `TRACK_DEFS` is a chord chart + tempo + seed + comping/drum style; `buildTrack` generates (deterministically) a walking bass, brass-style stabs, drums and a melody that follows the chords/scales; `musicTick()` schedules notes ahead on the audio clock. The title/cutscene theme (`home`, in C) and level 1 (`grove`, in F) use the chord changes and 156 swing tempo of Cab Calloway's "Everybody Eats When They Come to My House" (the game's inspiration); only the harmony is borrowed, the melodies are generated/original, so don't transcribe the song's melody or arrangement into the game (it's copyrighted). Levels name their track in `music` (`grove`, `deli`, ...); boss fights use `bossA/B/C`; cutscenes and the title use `home`.
- `game.js`: the engine: input, sound, state, Cab, enemies, the shared boss engine (`updateBoss`), cutscenes, HUD and the main loop.
- `PLAN.md`: roadmap and done list. Update it when features land.

## Conventions
- Logical resolution 320x180, drawn at 4x (`SS`) so text is crisp. Draw with the `R()` (rect), `disc()` and `sprite()` helpers and integer coordinates. All art is drawn in code; no image files.
- Tunables are in `TUNE` at the top of `game.js`. `loadLevel(i)` copies `LEVELS[i]` into globals (`PITS`, `PLATS`, `BELLS`, `MINIS`, `THROWERS`, `SLICERS`, `GEYSERS`, `PUDDLES`, `DROPS`, `ARENA`, `SEGS`, `DECOR`, `THEME`, `BOSS`, `LV`); `resetRun(x)` builds the runtime objects (`enemies`, `throwers`, `peels` (puddles), `drops`, `slicers`, `geysers`, `movers`, `boss`, ...).
- Level data: `pits` (the ground is the gaps between them), `plats` (one-way: `[x, topY, width]`), `movers` (boats: `[leftMin, leftMax, topY, width, speed, phase]`), `minis` (`[x, floorY, from, to, kind?]`: the optional kind overrides `kinds.mini`, so a level can mix them), `throwers` (`[x, platformTop]`), `slicers` (`[x, from, to]`), `geysers` (`[x, phase]`; `kinds.geyser` = `gravy` (default, a column from the floor) or `press` (a sandwich press that slams down)), `spikes` (`[x, width, 'thorns' | 'trap']`), `springs` (`[x]`: bouncy mushrooms; land on top to be launched ~110px; bonus ledges marked `'hi'` as a 4th `plats` entry are only reachable with one), `belts` (`[xFrom, xTo, dir]`: conveyor belts that push Cab along), `puddles`, `drops`, `bells` (checkpoints), `signs`, `arena`, and `cookies()`.
- `kinds` picks art and behavior: `mini` = `banana | salami | blob | tomato | mushroom | pepper | bread | pickle` (blobs, peppers and pickles hop at you; tomatoes roll at you when you come close; `chick` is only spawned by Count Cacciatore), `thrower` = `monkey | mouse | pod | gnome | hen | bottle` (whatever a thrower in `THROWER_PUDDLE` throws leaves a slippery puddle where it lands; the others' throws just burst), `drop` = `coconut | hook | cloud | vine | pinecone | cleaver`, `puddle` = `peel | oil | gravy | sauce | egg | mustard`. A new kind needs an entry in the matching art table (`MINI_ART`, `THROWER_ART` + `THROWN_ART`, `DROP_ART`, `drawPuddle`) and a color list (`MINI_COLORS`, `THROWER_COLORS`, `THROWN_COLORS`, `DROP_COLORS`).
- A new world needs a `THEMES` entry (see `deli` / `bayou`). New guests need `look` handling in `drawGuest` (`game.js`) and a food icon in `FOODS`.
- Feel (in `TUNE`): rising gravity .3, falling gravity .42 (heavier), jump 6.3, and letting go of jump caps the rise speed at `jumpCut` (so a tap is a short hop); letting go of the d-pad in the air brakes (`airFriction`) and turning in the air is quicker (`airTurn`). Full jump: apex ~63px, ~74px far at top speed; a quick tap ~32px. The camera (`updateCamera`) has a dead zone (`CAM_LEFT`/`CAM_RIGHT`) and moves in whole pixels; the loop snaps frame times to 30/60/120Hz so the 60Hz steps stay evenly spaced. Don't add easing back to the camera: it makes Cab shimmer on screen.
- Design rules (checked by a static script, see Testing): jump apex ~63px, max jump distance ~74px. Platform tops y >= 92; pits <= 56px unless a boat crosses them (boats should tuck ~8px over each bank and pause there). Keep checkpoints on solid ground, 25px+ from pits and 45px+ from enemy patrols, slicers, geysers; put a bell right before each boat lake. A puddle needs ~105px of ground ahead of it before a pit (a slide is ~80px; you can still jump). Geysers: 60px+ from pits and never under a platform (the column is 84px tall). Throwers must stand on a platform. Keep cookies out of the boss arena. Spikes: 60px+ from pits and 45px+ from bells; a puddle must not slide you (~80px) into spikes or a geyser/press. Springs: 100px+ from pits (the launch carries you) and one under each `'hi'` ledge. Belts: stay inside one ground segment and never push toward a pit within 100px. Presses: no platform within 26px. Boat lakes: players may wait at a bank for the boat, so keep shooters (throwers) 230px+ away from both banks and enemy patrols 70px+ away.
- Cab is `cab` (feet position `x,y`, 10x22 hitbox). Stomp = landing from above on an enemy (`killMini`, `killThrower`, the boss). Hurt = lose a heart + invulnerability. Pits cost a heart and respawn at the last bell; 0 hearts shows the retry screen (boss HP persists). `cab.ride` is the boat Cab is standing on (he's carried by its `dx`).
- Boss engine (`updateBoss`): crossing `ARENA.trigger` starts the fight, drops a crate wall and locks the camera at `ARENA.left`. Generic states: `sleep, intro, hurt, dazed, dying, dead`; everything else (`walk` and his attacks) is the boss's own `step()`. A boss defines `name`, `tip` (shown on the intro banner: say how to hurt him), `h`/`hw` (stompable top + half-width), `dangerous(b)`, `stompable(b)`, optional `box(b)` (hitbox override, e.g. the rolling salami) and `freezeFace(b)`. The top ~45% of his hitbox (max 24px) is his head: landing on it while he's stompable is a hit, and brushing it is harmless; his body below hurts. While he's dizzy (`dazed`) a bouncing "STOMP!" arrow shows (`drawStompCue`). His phase (0-2) grows as he's hurt. After he dies a golden food drops; grabbing it starts the ending (outro dialogue -> unlock card -> next guest).
- Bosses and what's special about them: `peel` (Baron Von Peel: leaps and slams, slides peels), `salami` (slices at two heights, rolls like a wheel, dizzy at the wall), `gravy` (lobs gravy at marked spots, pours a wave, empty and dizzy), `tomato` (Sir Splatsalot: tosses stompable mini tomatoes, bounces 2-3 times spraying seeds, then is splatted flat), `chicken` (Count Cacciatore: sends chicks along the floor from both walls, flies off-screen while a shadow tracks you, dives head-first and is stuck upside-down), `baloney` (King Baloney: crown boomerang; and two slices of bread slide in from both walls: dodge them and they squash him, which is his dizzy window). A boss can spawn minions into `enemies` (chicks are cleared when he's hit) and keep any state he likes on `boss`.
- Peel/puddle objects (`peels`) are generic slippery puddles: bosses and throwers can add to them. Touching one starts a ~36-frame slide (`cab.slip`): he stays on the ground, drifts forward and can still jump; `drawCab(..., {slip})` draws the skid pose (feet forward, leaning back, arms windmilling) and `updateCab` kicks up dust. Don't rotate the sprite: it looked chaotic.
- Sound: all synthesized (`tone`, `noise`, `sfx` in `game.js`; the music in `music.js`). To change how a track feels, edit its `TRACK_DEFS` entry (`bpm`, `prog` chords like `'C6 | A7 | Dm7 G7'` (a bar with two chords = two half bars), `seed` (re-rolls the melody), `comp` = `charleston | chops | shout | sparse`, `drums` = `swing | brush | drive`, `density`, `blues`); overall loudness is `MUSIC_GAIN`. A bar is 12 ticks (triplet grid), and swung eighths are a 2-tick note followed by a 1-tick note. M mutes (saved in localStorage). Level progress is saved as `ee-progress`.
- Touch: use pointer events only; zoom is blocked on purpose (same as the Boulder game).

## Running and testing
- `python3 -m http.server 8000`, then open http://localhost:8000. Debug URLs: `?level=N` plus `&play`, `&boss`, `&intro`, `&end`, `&unlock`, `&x=NNNN`.
- `window.__game` exposes state for Playwright tests (`state`, `cab`, `boss`, `LV`, `step()`, `input`, `setInput`, `setBossHp`, `enemies`, `movers`, ...). A bot that holds right, holds jump through the rise, jumps at pit edges, waits for geysers, and boards/hops off boats can play through each level and beat the boss (refill `hearts` each frame so only pits matter). Quick static checks of the layout rules above are easy to script against `LEVELS`.
- Test both landscape and portrait phone viewports (touch buttons appear under the canvas in portrait).
